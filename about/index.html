Enviromental impact

Hosting any website or requesting data on the internet costs electricity. For this reason, this poorly optimised hobby site passively emits about 100 to 200 grams of CO₂ per year (enqual to charging a phone 25 times) and another ~0.3 grams per visit. Since it is powered by GitHub Pages and GitHub Actions, which are owned by Microsoft, the net emittions are 0 grams of CO₂.

One ChatGPT prompt emits about 0,17 to 1 gram of CO₂, and the training of AI in general is about 32,6 to 79,7 million tons per year as of 2026.
